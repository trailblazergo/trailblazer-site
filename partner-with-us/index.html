Oklahoma

Osage Nation

Trailblazer supports high school counselors from the Osage Nation, a federally recognized sovereign nation, serving roughly 1,500 Osage Nation students across Oklahoma.

Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Esperanza Cyber Charter School

Trailblazer supports the counseling team of the Esperanza Cyber Charter School, a K-12 school serving students in North Philadelphia.

Playa del Carmen, Quintana Roo, Mexico

Keeping Kids in School Project (KKIS)

Trailblazer partners with the Keeping Kids in School Project, a nonprofit in Playa del Carmen, Mexico, to enable students to learn more about attending university across Mexico from KKIS alumni.

Virginia

Virginia Tribal Education Consortium (VTEC)

Trailblazer is fully available for the seven federally recognized sovereign Tribes of Virginia: Chickahominy Indian Tribe, Chickahominy Indian Tribe Eastern Division, Monacan Indian Nation, Nansemond Indian Nation, Upper Mattaponi Tribe, Pamunkey Indian Tribe, and The Rappahannock Indian Tribe.
