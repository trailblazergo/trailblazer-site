What can you ask an advisor?

You can ask your advisors anything you'd ask a trusted friend or older sibling. Ask about topics like:

  • Finishing high school & building college list
  • College applications & essays
  • College transition & first-gen life
  • Academics, extra-curriculars, & leadership
  • Career & academic pathways
  • Managing finances & scholarships
  • Community & connecting to home
